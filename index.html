2. Manual inputs — not part of the salary sheets (optional)

Leave blank if not available — the Sales Total then excludes this block and the export carries a visible warning.

Hold review

Employees with a Remark in the salary sheet. Remarks Resigned / Absconding / Hold / ATL / Terminate / Accident are held by default (their salary Net Pay is held back). Tick/untick to match payroll's actual holds.

Loan split — Principal / Interest

Loan rows of the HOLD&TDS → Deductions block. Enter the Principal and Interest of each loan EMI as per the loan register (filled automatically if the salary sheet has Principal / Interest columns). Leave blank to keep the cell empty.
